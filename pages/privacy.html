Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Discord Activity Sync · By TaskStalker

TL;DR

Discord Activity Sync processes your active tab title and URL locally on your device to display Discord Rich Presence activity. No data is transmitted to external servers, collected, stored, tracked, sold, or monetized.

Overview

Discord Activity Sync is a Firefox browser extension developed by TaskStalker. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is processed, and how the companion desktop application interacts with the Discord desktop client.

Information Accessed

The extension may access the following browser information:

  • The URL of your currently active tab
  • The title of your currently active tab
  • Real-time title updates while remaining on the same page
  • Supported website metadata used for Rich Presence display

This information is used solely to display your current browsing activity as Discord Rich Presence on your local Discord desktop client.

How the System Works

The extension communicates only with a companion desktop application running locally on your device through localhost:3031.

The companion desktop application then communicates with the Discord desktop client using Discord’s local Rich Presence IPC interface.

This architecture is required because web browsers cannot directly access Discord’s RPC interface.

How Your Data Is Used

The extension sends tab metadata as a local JSON payload to the companion desktop application running on your machine.

  • All processing occurs entirely on the user's device
  • No information is transmitted to external servers
  • No cloud infrastructure or remote databases are used
  • No telemetry, analytics, or tracking systems exist
  • No browsing data is stored, logged, retained, or shared
  • No data is sold or monetized in any way

The companion desktop application only accepts localhost connections and is not intended for remote or public network access.

Data We Do Not Collect

TaskStalker does not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Browsing history
  • Passwords or form inputs
  • Cookies or authentication tokens
  • Discord account credentials
  • Personal information
  • Analytics or telemetry data
  • Usage statistics

Permissions Explained

  • tabs — Required to access the active tab title and URL
  • http://localhost:3031/* — Required to communicate with the local companion desktop application

No additional permissions are requested or used.

Third-Party Services

The extension communicates only with the Discord desktop client running on your local machine.

Discord’s own privacy policies apply to how Discord handles Rich Presence activity. See discord.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed toward children under the age of 13. No information is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected by the updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of any revisions.

Contact
